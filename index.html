Event FAQs

A few things before you gather

What kind of gathering works well at Indie?

Indie welcomes team gatherings, founder and investor conversations, client receptions, private dinners, celebrations, wine experiences, all have a place here. Tell us what you’re planning, including any presentation or tasting, so we can discuss the right space.

How easy is it to park?

Street parking is available around Green and Sansome Streets, and guests often find this quieter neighborhood convenient for an evening visit. Check the signs where you park, including meter hours and time limits. For your invitation, use our address: 175 Green Street, San Francisco.

Where is it located?

Indie Wine & Beer Bar at SF Wine Center is located on Green Street at Sansome, steps from Levi Plaza in the North Beach / Telegraph Hill neighborhood. Nearby landmarks include the Exploratorium at Pier 15 and Cobb’s Comedy Club.

What makes the wine selection at Indie different?

The pleasure is in discovering wines you don’t often find by the glass, including bottles you might otherwise only encounter on a collector’s wine list. Alongside familiar regions, there are less familiar grapes and producers worth getting to know.

What kind of wines do you serve?

Our extensive wine list brings together New World and Old World producers, with reds, whites, rosés, Champagne, and sparkling wines available by the glass and bottle. Choose something familiar, build a flight, or ask us to help you discover a new favorite.

Is the patio covered? What happens if it rains?

The patio is uncovered, with comfortable lounge and table seating around the fire pit and beneath heaters. Plan for rain.

Is there a rental fee and a food and beverage minimum?

Private events for more than 15 guests have a space rental fee and a separate food and beverage minimum, depending on the space and group size. Standard rentals have a three-hour minimum. Regular table reservations for 15 or fewer have neither during opening hours.

Can we book when the bar is normally closed?

Yes. Groups of 35 or more can arrange events on days the bar would normally be closed, subject to availability. Cooking classes and combined cooking-and-wine experiences also have separate scheduling options. Share your preferred date, time, and guest count so the team can recommend an arrangement.
